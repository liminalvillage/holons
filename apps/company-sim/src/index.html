    How it works

    1. WorkPeople finish tasks, publish articles and thank each other. Tasks can travel to other circles as holograms — live pointers, never copies.
    2. EquationEvery circle scores its members by its own value equation: what the circle chooses to reward.
    3. CascadeMoney sent to a holon is split: part to its own members by score, the rest to circles on its rings, which split it again.
    4. ClaimEach person's share becomes a right they can claim. Pot funds are collected after the new moon commit.
