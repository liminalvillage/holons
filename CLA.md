## How we collect agreement to this CLA

Practically, the CLA is accepted in one of these ways (a maintainer will tell
you which applies; the default is the first):

1. **CLA assistant bot** — on your first pull request, an automated check
   ([cla-assistant](https://github.com/cla-assistant/cla-assistant) or
   equivalent) posts a one-time link; clicking "I agree" with your GitHub
   identity records your acceptance for all future PRs.
2. **DCO-style sign-off** — adding `Signed-off-by: Name <email>` to each commit
   (`git commit -s`), where the sign-off additionally indicates acceptance of
   this CLA as stated in [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md).
3. **Manual** — for substantial or entity contributions, emailing a signed copy
   of this Agreement to we@holons.io.

If no CLA automation is configured yet, opening a pull request constitutes Your
acceptance of this CLA for the Contribution in that pull request, and you should
add `Signed-off-by:` to your commits.
