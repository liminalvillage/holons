🗳️ How Democratic Holons Work

Every decision space is a democratic holon—simultaneously autonomous and nested within larger governance structures. Your neighborhood votes on local issues independently, while regional decisions automatically federate input from all neighborhoods.

This is fundamentally different from representative democracy. With VoiceVillage, geography is governance. Spatial relationships define decision-making boundaries. This means:

  • Vote on issues that directly affect your place
  • Automatic representation in higher-level decisions
  • Natural data sovereignty and privacy boundaries
  • Offline voting with eventual consensus
