🔧 How Tool Holons Work

Each tool is a holon—a geospatial entity that knows where it is, who owns it, and who needs it. Tools automatically become discoverable to neighbors within your chosen radius.

This isn't a marketplace—it's a mesh. No platform fees, no extraction, no surveillance. Tools find borrowers, borrowers find tools, and communities build resilience through reciprocity.

  • Instant "show me all drills within 5 blocks" queries
  • Automatic notifications when tools you need become available
  • Offline-first design works without internet
  • Community data stays in community hands
