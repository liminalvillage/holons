Construction waste fills landfills while builders pay premium prices for virgin materials. Reclaimed material markets are fragmented with no way to match supply with nearby demand or track environmental impact.

🌐 How Holons Work

Each material listing is a holon—an H3 geographic cell containing supply that can match with demand in neighboring cells. Your oak beams automatically relate to nearby projects, which relate to regional building patterns.

This is fundamentally different from traditional marketplaces. With HoloSphere, geography is the database. Proximity relationships are native, not calculated. This means:

  • Instant "show me materials within economic transport radius" queries
  • Automatic clustering of compatible materials for projects
  • Natural boundaries for regional material economies
  • Real-time coordination across deconstruction crews
