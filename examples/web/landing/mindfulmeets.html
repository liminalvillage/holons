🌐 How Spiritual Holons Work

Each meditation group, prayer circle, or spiritual gathering is a holon—an autonomous community that can optionally federate with others. Your practice maintains complete sovereignty while discovering resonance with neighbors.

This means:

  • Find practice by location: "Show me all meditation sits within 15 minutes walk"
  • Respect boundaries: Closed practices stay closed. Open circles welcome newcomers.
  • Bridge traditions: Opt-in interfaith connection when it serves the practice
  • Track lineages: Know the authentic teachers and protect against appropriation
  • Collective healing: Multiple traditions focusing intention on shared needs

🛡️ Protecting Sacred Traditions

Cultural appropriation is spiritual theft. MindfulMeets includes fine-grained access controls so Indigenous ceremonies, closed practices, and sacred knowledge remain protected.

Communities decide who can see their gatherings, who can attend, and how their tradition is represented. Sovereignty first, connection second.

The Dana Model

Honoring ancient traditions where spiritual practice is never commodified

100% Donation-Based

🎁

Dana (Pali for "generosity") is the ancient Buddhist practice of freely offering teachings. In this spirit, MindfulMeets is completely free.

If it serves your practice, you can offer dana to support the infrastructure. No one is excluded. No one profits. Just practice.

🏘️ Integration with NeighborHub

Spiritual communities need physical space. MindfulMeets integrates with NeighborHub (community space sharing) so groups can find churches, community centers, homes, and parks to gather.

No rental fees. No platform extraction. Just neighbors sharing space for contemplative practice.
