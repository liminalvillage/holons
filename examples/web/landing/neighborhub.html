🌐 How Holons Work

Each shared space is a holon—an H3 geographic cell that acts as both an autonomous unit and part of larger neighborhood hierarchies. Your backyard automatically relates to your block, which relates to your neighborhood.

This is fundamentally different from traditional booking platforms. With HoloSphere, geography is the database. Location relationships are native, not bolted on. This means:

  • Instant "show me spaces within 15 minutes" queries
  • Automatic neighborhood federation
  • Natural community boundaries for governance
  • Offline-first with local resilience
