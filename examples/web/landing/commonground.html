🏞️ How Land Holons Work

Every parcel is a land holon—an H3 geographic cell containing tenure, ecology, and stewardship data. Each holon is simultaneously an autonomous unit and part of larger bioregional hierarchies. Your meadow relates to your watershed, which relates to your bioregion.

This is fundamentally different from GIS databases. With CommonGround, geography is governance. Spatial relationships define stewardship responsibilities. This means:

  • Instant "show me all parcels in this watershed" queries
  • Automatic upstream/downstream stewardship coordination
  • Natural data sovereignty for Indigenous territories
  • Offline stewardship with eventual synchronization
