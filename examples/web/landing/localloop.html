🌐 How Currency Holons Work

Each LocalLoop is a currency holon—an H3 geographic cell that acts as its own monetary zone while federating with neighbors. Your neighborhood currency automatically relates to your city, which can relate to regional exchanges.

This is fundamentally different from cryptocurrency or traditional banking. With HoloSphere, geography defines the monetary boundary. This means:

  • Instant visibility into local economic health
  • Automatic trade between adjacent communities
  • Democratic control over your monetary zone
  • Offline-first transactions with eventual settlement
  • No blockchain mining waste—just pure circulation
