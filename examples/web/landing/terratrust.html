🌐 How Holons Work

Each monitoring site is a holon—an H3 geographic cell that acts as both an autonomous unit and part of larger hierarchies. Your meadow observations automatically relate to your watershed, which relates to your bioregion.

This is fundamentally different from traditional databases. With HoloSphere, geography is the database. Spatial relationships are native, not bolted on. This means:

  • Instant "show me all observations within 5km" queries
  • Automatic upstream/downstream relationships
  • Natural data sovereignty boundaries
  • Offline-first with eventual consistency
