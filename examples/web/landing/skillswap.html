🤝 How Skill Holons Work

Each skill is a holon—a geospatial entity that knows who has it, who needs it, and how they connect. Skills automatically become discoverable to neighbors within your chosen radius.

This creates a parallel economy outside capitalism. Your grandmother's lifetime of caregiving wisdom becomes as valuable as a software engineer's code. Time is the universal currency.

  • Instant "who can teach me carpentry?" queries
  • Automatic reciprocity path finding
  • Federate with LearnLocal for formal teaching
  • Integrate with LocalLoop for asymmetric exchanges
