🌐 How Cultural Holons Work

Each story is a holon—connected to place, language, speaker, and time. Your grandmother's story about the salmon run automatically relates to the river holon, the fishing holon, the seasonal holon, and the family lineage holon.

This is fundamentally different from traditional archives. With StoryCircle, place is memory. Geographic relationships preserve cultural context. This means:

  • Stories heard where they happened
  • Automatic seasonal and ecological connections
  • Community sovereignty over sacred knowledge
  • Offline-first for remote communities
