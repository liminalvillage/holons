## Who needs this

Holons is available to everyone for free under the
[GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later](./LICENSE.md). You may want a commercial license if any
of the following apply:

- You want to incorporate Holons (or a modified version) into a **proprietary,
  closed-source product** and not release your modifications.
- You operate a **hosted/SaaS** offering built on a modified Holons and do not
  want to disclose your modifications to your users as AGPL §13 requires.
- Your organization's policy or contractual obligations **forbid AGPL**
  components.
- You need warranties, indemnification, support SLAs, or other terms the AGPL
  explicitly disclaims.

If none of these apply, you do **not** need a commercial license — use Holons
under the AGPL.

## What a commercial license typically grants

The exact terms are set per agreement, but a commercial license generally
provides:

- A non-exclusive right to use, modify, and distribute Holons **without** the
  AGPL's source-disclosure and network-use (§13) obligations.
- The right to sublicense Holons as part of your own product, in binary or
  source form, under your own terms.
- Optionally: priority support, a defined release/maintenance window, and
  warranty/indemnification terms.

## What it does not change

- The open-source AGPL version remains available to everyone, unchanged.
- Contributions to the public project remain under the AGPL and the
  [CLA](./CLA.md).
- A commercial license is granted only by the Licensor and only in writing.

## How to obtain one

Email **we@holons.io** with:

1. Your organization and the product/service you intend to build.
2. How you intend to use Holons (embedded, hosted, distributed, …).
3. Approximate scale (users/seats/instances) so terms can be sized fairly.

You will receive a proposed agreement. No commercial rights exist until that
agreement is signed by the Licensor.
