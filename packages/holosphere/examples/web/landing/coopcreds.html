🌐 How Credit Holons Work

Each credit circle is a trust holon—a self-sovereign financial community that can federate with neighboring circles for risk pooling and emergency lending. Your circle's reputation propagates through the network.

This is fundamentally different from banks or even blockchain finance. With HoloSphere, social relationships are the infrastructure. This means:

  • Zero interest because money is mutual aid, not commodity
  • Community accountability instead of legal enforcement
  • Reputation earned through participation, not credit scores
  • Offline-first operations—no internet required
  • Data sovereignty—your financial history stays with your community
