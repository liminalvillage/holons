🌐 How Healing Holons Work

Each healing modality, practitioner, and wellness space is a holon—a geospatial entity that relates to everything around it. Your neighborhood's healers automatically connect to broader networks while maintaining independence.

This means:

  • Discover healing by location: "Show me all herbalists within 2 miles"
  • Follow healing paths: See what modalities people combine for specific conditions
  • Track affordability: Real-time sliding scale availability across your area
  • Protect sacred knowledge: Traditional practices shared only with permission
  • Build healing communities: Practitioners collaborate without platform extraction
